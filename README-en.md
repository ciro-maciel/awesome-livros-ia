## To get started

What everyone needs to know before using AI at work.

- [IA no dia a dia (vol. 1)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-no-dia-a-dia-vol-1) - What everyone needs to know about artificial intelligence to work. `paid` `by author`
- [IA por dentro (vol. 2)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-por-dentro-vol-2) - How the machines that talk to us work. `paid` `by author`

## By profession

A volume per trade, with daily tasks and professional rules. Each one has an open online kit.

- [IA no escritório (vol. 3)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-no-escritorio-vol-3) - For secretaries, assistants and administrative aides. `paid` `by author`
- [IA no primeiro trabalho (vol. 4)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-no-primeiro-trabalho-vol-4) - From job offer to first payslip, for those just starting out. `paid` `by author`
- [IA no pequeno negócio (vol. 5)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-no-pequeno-negocio-vol-5) - From WhatsApp to the cash register, for the MEI and small business owners. `paid` `by author`
- [IA no atendimento e nas vendas (vol. 6)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-no-atendimento-e-nas-vendas-vol) - For salespeople, cashiers, receptionists and telemarketing operators. `paid` `by author`
- [IA na sala de aula (vol. 7)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-na-sala-de-aula-vol-7) - For teachers: lesson plans, grading, adapted activities and reports without exposing the student. `paid` `by author`
- [IA na contabilidade (vol. 8)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-na-contabilidade-vol-8) - For accountants, technicians and assistants: from bank statements to DCTFWeb, without exposing the client. `paid` `by author`
- [IA na advocacia (vol. 9)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-ia-na-advocacia-vol-9) - For lawyers: petitions, case law and deadlines, without fake rulings or exposing the client. `paid` `by author`

## For builders

Systems, products and companies built with AI agents.

- [Construir com IA (vol. 10)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-construir-com-ia-vol-10) - From prototype to live system: login, data and billing for people who build without knowing how to code. `paid` `by author`
- [Feito para agentes (vol. 11)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-feito-para-agentes-vol-11) - How to make your customer's AI agent use your product: MCP server, llms.txt and authorization. `paid` `by author`
- [A empresa de uma pessoa (vol. 12)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-a-empresa-de-uma-pessoa-vol-12) - One Person Business: how to set up and run a whole business with AI agents. `paid` `by author`
- [Do primeiro visitante ao cliente (vol. 13)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-do-primeiro-visitante-ao-cliente-v) - How to set up, with AI agents, your storefront, lead generation and customer relationships. `paid` `by author`
- [Forward Deployed Engineer (vol. 14)](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-forward-deployed-engineer-vol-14) - How to become the engineer who takes AI from pilot to production. `paid` `by author`

## To understand the debate

Brazilian editions of books by other authors on what AI changes. In alphabetical order.

- [Cointeligência](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-cointeligencia) - Ethan Mollick, professor at Wharton, on living and working with AI as a colleague. Intrínseca. `paid`
- [Nexus](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-nexus) - Yuval Noah Harari tells the story of information networks, from the Stone Age to AI. Companhia das Letras. `paid`
- [A próxima onda](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-a-proxima-onda) - Mustafa Suleyman and Michael Bhaskar on AI, power and the biggest dilemma of the century. Record. `paid`
- [Superinteligência](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-superinteligencia) - Nick Bostrom on the paths, dangers and strategies facing an AI more capable than we are. DarkSide. `paid`
- [Vida 3.0](https://go.mymarketing.click/livia-vida-3-0) - Max Tegmark on human beings in the age of artificial intelligence. Benvirá. `paid`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags beside it help you choose: official when it comes from the people who set the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid according to the usage model, and API and SDK according to how you integrate. Items tagged "by author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.

## How to contribute

Know something that is missing, has changed, or is gone? Open an issue with the new entry form or send a pull request editing data/list.json. The README for each language is generated from this file.
